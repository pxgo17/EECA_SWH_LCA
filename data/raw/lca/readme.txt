The majority of EPD data was from the PEP ecopassport 
http://www.pep-ecopassport.org/
Access date: 23 January 2025

A summary of the EPDs used is in the EECA Residential Space and Water Heating Study is provided
PEPecopassport_product_filtered.xlsx
PEPecopassport_functionalunit_filtered.xlsx

These files were created from data scraped from the PEP ecopassport for the appliances of interest.
The two files represent data given at the product level, and at the functional unit level.

This is the (largely) uncleaned data, cleaning was undertaken at a later step to correct obvious data input errors.

manufacture.csv has processed data that was used for linear model fitting, and supporting coefficient analysis for manufacturing locations, weight, and ratings as independent variables.
